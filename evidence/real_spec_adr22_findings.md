One run of the Dark Matter Engine on a design document I did not write.

- **Document:** ADR-22, "JetStream Publish Retries on No Responders", from
  https://github.com/nats-io/nats-architecture-and-design (Apache License 2.0). The document is not
  copied into this repo. Short quotes below are the quotes the engine cited, with attribution.
- **Settings:** defaults, as frozen (`python dark_matter.py scan <folder>`), `gemma3:4b`, digest `68ceab7e468a`,
  run on Oct 23, 2026. Scenarios were generated by the model. One run, nothing tuned.
- **Engine's own summary:** 12 scenarios, 0 potential gaps, 0 needing review. (Its one "Partially answered"
  finding is not counted as a potential gap.)

## Tally

- A, correct and supported: **3** (G2, G8, G9)
- B, genuine unanswered question: **1** (G10)
- C, Answered but the quote does not support it: **5** (G1, G3, G4, G7, G12)
- D, scenario contradicts or is not about the spec: **3** (G5, G6, G11)

So 4 of 12 findings hold up and 8 do not. The one genuine finding was labeled Partially answered, not
Not established. This is the same failure as the worker-crash case in the benchmark: a real quote that does
not establish the claim. The citation check cannot catch it, and the default relevance check let most of
these through.
